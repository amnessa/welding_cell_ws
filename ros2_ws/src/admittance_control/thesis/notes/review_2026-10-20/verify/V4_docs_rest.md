@S 3.5.3 | Mask x depth is an element-wise (Hadamard) product and still an image; back-projection X=(u−cx)Z/fx, Y=(v−cy)Z/fy gives the N×3 matrix (what the advisor called "3×N", transposed), in the camera frame — RP §2a-2b (written to answer MEET [00:03:20]-[00:08:41])
@S 3.5.3 | Why each cleaning step: erode 2 px (silhouette pixels mix part and background depth into a "skirt" that does not exist); normals on a 4 px dilated region, then the rim dropped (a border point's neighbours lie on one side, its normal tips inward); normals flipped toward the camera because CAD normals point outward and the angle features are sign-sensitive; voxel max(D/60, 1.5 mm), at most 2000 points ("more only costs time") — RP §2c (= PA, B2)
@S 3.5.3 | CAD to model cloud: a PLY is a triangle mesh, not a cloud (`test_objv2_ear.ply` = 8 vertices, 12 triangles, a box: 8 points would match nothing); 60 000 area-uniform samples (`trimesh.sample_surface`) with the exact face normal, thinned to ~600 by keeping one representative per voxel, not the mean ("averaging would round off the box's corners and create 45° normals ... that belong to neither"); the mesh need not be watertight (area sampling) but its normals must point outward — RP §Preparation
@S 3.5.3 | PPF for a reader: the 4 numbers describe a PAIR of points, not a point; they are hash keys, not a matrix ("There is no such matrix; I explained it wrongly in the meeting"); OpenCV cannot save the table, so every model is retrained at start-up (~1-2 s per model) and `Data/ppf_library.npz` holds only the sampled clouds; matching is by voting, so point order and count do not matter — RP §3a-3c, §Answers
@S 3.5.3 | ICP inside PPF verification, and why PPF is still needed: ICP needs a good start; PPF voting provides it and 50 ICP iterations remove the error of the 12° angle bins (a few mm); the score is computed on the corrected pose — RP §Answers "Is there ICP inside?"
